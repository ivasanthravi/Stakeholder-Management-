## Proposed help-center structure
1. **Getting started** — Create your account · Verify your email · Create a workspace and choose a template · Guided setup · Invite your team · Roles and permissions · Your first stakeholder, engagement and commitment · The Command Center · Keyboard shortcuts
2. **Stakeholders & organizations** — Stakeholder directory · Influence, stance and interest · Organizations · Restricted stakeholders · Archiving
3. **Initiatives** — Creating initiatives · Linking stakeholders · Renaming vocabulary
4. **Relationship mapping** — Recording relationships · The network map · Influence grid · Find path
5. **Engagements, decisions & commitments** — Logging engagements · Decision workflow · Tracking commitments · Overdue and due-soon
6. **AI features** — How AI works in Meridian (rules engine vs model) · Meeting intelligence · Reviewing proposals · Briefings · Copilot · AI allowance and limits
7. **Notifications** — Inbox · Daily digest · Email preferences and unsubscribe
8. **Account & security** — Password reset · Email verification · Sessions · Closing your account
9. **Workspace administration** — Members and invitations · Seats · Audit log · AI settings · Data export · Deleting a workspace
10. **Billing** — Trials · Plans and seats · Checkout · Changing plan or seats · Cancel and resume · Failed payments · Read-only workspaces · Invoices
11. **Privacy & security** — Security controls · AI data handling · Legal documents (once published)
12. **Known issues** — [known-issues.md](known-issues.md)
